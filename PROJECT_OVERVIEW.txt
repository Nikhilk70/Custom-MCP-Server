AUTO-JOB-AGENT  (web app name: ApplyPilot)
How the project works, in plain words
=====================================================================


1. WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?
---------------------------------------------------------------------
AUTO-JOB-AGENT is a personal job-search assistant that runs on your own
computer. It is built for the Indian job market (Bengaluru, Pune,
Hyderabad, Mumbai, remote and so on) and for fresher / 0-2 year roles
such as Java Full Stack, Backend, Full Stack and Software Engineer.

It does five jobs for you:

  1. FINDS jobs        - searches company career pages and job boards.
  2. CHECKS jobs       - reads each job description (JD) and decides
                         whether you are really eligible for it.
  3. TAILORS resumes   - builds a resume for each good job, using only
                         true facts from your own data.
  4. FILLS applications- opens the job's application form in Chrome and
                         fills it in for you.
  5. KEEPS records     - saves everything in a Google Sheet so you can
                         see every job, status and result.

It does not lie, guess or bypass security. When it is unsure, it stops
and asks you.


2. THE BIG PICTURE (how a job moves through the system)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

   Company career pages / job boards
                |
                v
   [1] DISCOVER   -> collect job posts
                |
                v
   [2] CLEAN UP   -> normalise and remove duplicates
                |
                v
   [3] READ JD    -> fetch the full job description
                |
                v
   [4] MATCH      -> technical score (0-100%)
                |    + hard eligibility check (PASS / FAIL)
                |
         FAIL --+--> NOT_ELIGIBLE (stop, with the exact reason)
                |
              PASS
                v
   [5] RESUME     -> tailored resume made from your data (LaTeX -> PDF)
                |
                v
   [6] APPLY      -> open the form, fill it, answer questions
                |
                v
   [7] YOU CHECK  -> you review, solve CAPTCHA/OTP yourself, approve
                |
                v
   [8] SUBMIT     -> submitted, then checked: confirmed or not?
                |
                v
   [9] RECORD     -> Google Sheet + reports updated


3. STEP BY STEP
---------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 - Discover jobs
  * Your company list is in the "Companies/" folder (32 companies:
    MNCs, product companies, service companies, startups).
  * For each company the agent uses its job system (Greenhouse, Lever,
    Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday ...) or Firecrawl/LinkedIn where set up.
  * Every job keeps both its listing link and its real apply link, so
    the two are never mixed up.

STEP 2 - Clean up
  * Company names, titles and locations are normalised.
  * The same job found twice (for example on LinkedIn and on the
    company site) is marked DUPLICATE and kept only once.

STEP 3 - Read the job description
  * The full JD is fetched. If no JD can be found, the job is not
    scored. The agent never invents a JD.

STEP 4 - Match (two separate checks)
  a) Technical match score (0-100%), from:
       required skills, preferred skills, job title, experience,
       location, work mode and education.
  b) Hard eligibility gate. It checks in this order:
       role -> experience -> must-have requirements -> minimum score
  * A high score does NOT make a job eligible. Example:
       "Technical match 82% but needs 5+ years" -> NOT_ELIGIBLE.
  * Only skills that are in your own data (MY_DATA) earn points.

STEP 5 - Tailored resume
  * Starts from your master LaTeX template.
  * Changes only the allowed sections: it reorders your real skills,
    projects and bullet points to fit the JD.
  * It never adds a skill or experience you do not have.
  * The resume is compiled to PDF and checked before use.

STEP 6 - Apply
  * Chrome opens with its own saved profile, and the agent finds the
    application form.
  * Every field is filled from a trusted source:
       your profile, your resume, Application_Answers.txt, the
       question bank, or an answer you typed in during the run.
  * If a required question has no known answer, the agent STOPS and
    asks you. It never guesses. You can save your answer for next
    time ("remember") or use it once only.

STEP 7 - Human checkpoints (always you)
  * CAPTCHA, OTP, MFA, login, identity checks -> you do them.
  * Payment request -> hard stop. The agent never pays.
  * Before submitting, you see a review of every field and approve it.

STEP 8 - Submit and verify
  * After submitting, the agent looks for proof:
       VERIFIED   - confirmation message or page found
       UNVERIFIED - submitted but no confirmation seen
       REJECTED   - the site showed an error (for example "error
                    verifying your application")
  * It never reports "applied" without evidence.

STEP 9 - Record
  * The Google Sheet gets the job's status, match score, reason,
    resume version, attempts, errors and timeline.


4. JOB STATUSES (state machine)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  DISCOVERED -> NORMALIZED -> JD_FETCHED -> MATCHED / NOT_MATCHED
  -> READY_FOR_RESUME -> RESUME_GENERATED -> READY_TO_APPLY
  -> APPLYING -> FORM_DETECTED -> FORM_FILLED -> SUBMITTING
  -> APPLIED  or  SUBMISSION_UNVERIFIED

  Side states: NEEDS_HUMAN, VALIDATION_FAILED, FAILED, BLOCKED,
               SKIPPED, DUPLICATE

  Only allowed moves are accepted, and every move is logged with a reason.


5. SAFETY RULES (built in, cannot be switched off from the app)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Default mode is DRY RUN / REVIEW ONLY: forms are filled but nothing
    is submitted.
  * LIVE mode must be turned on by you: type "LIVE" to arm it, then
    confirm "LIVE" again for each run. There is also a daily limit.
  * Never bypasses or auto-clicks CAPTCHA, OTP or MFA.
  * Never types or stores your Google or site passwords.
  * Never fabricates answers, skills, experience or results.
  * Never applies to the same job twice (duplicate protection).
  * Secrets live only in .env and config/google-credentials.json, and
    both are git-ignored.
  * STOP ALL, pause and skip buttons are always available.

8. RAG ("Ask your data")
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Builds a small search index from YOUR records only: answers,
    profile, experience, projects, skills and resume text.
  * Search uses keyword (BM25) + Gemini embeddings together.
  * Sensitive items are never sent to the AI.
  * It SUGGESTS answers with sources. You must accept a suggestion;
    it is never auto-filled.
  * Safety checks reject suggestions that are not backed by your data
    (for example a number that appears in none of the sources).

10. WHAT IS BEING BUILT NOW - "AI Career Engineering System"
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: don't just apply to more jobs - help you BECOME qualified for
the right ones.

  Loop: DISCOVER -> UNDERSTAND -> MEASURE -> IMPROVE -> BUILD EVIDENCE
        -> APPLY -> PREPARE -> LEARN -> REMEMBER -> EVOLVE

    internships, projects, skills, certifications, achievements, GitHub,

DONE AND TESTED (1 Oct 2026) - Phase 2: Career Memory
  * A permanent, versioned memory of your career, stored on your PC in
    runtime/state/applypilot/career-memory/.
  * It holds 14 kinds of records:
      Profile, Skills, Projects, Education, Experience, Certifications,
      Resumes, Applications, Questions, Interviews, Learning, Evidence,
      Companies, Outcomes.
  * Stable IDs that never change for the same thing (SKILL-xxxxxxxx,
    APP-xxxxxxxx, RESUME-..., INTERVIEW-...).
  * Versioning: every change becomes version 2, 3, ... and every old
    version can still be opened. Nothing is overwritten; a deleted record
    keeps its history and can be restored.
  * Timestamps (created / last changed), the source of every version,
    and evidence links between records (for example
    skill -> internship / project; application -> resume).
  * How it updates:
      - Synced automatically when the app starts, on refresh, and after
        every job search and every apply run.
      - Synced records come from MY_DATA, your Google Sheet and
        Companies/. Edit the source and memory records the change.
      - Interviews, learning, outcomes and evidence are yours alone:
        the system never invents or changes them. They are added through
        the API for now; screens for them come with the later phases.
  * Where to see it: ApplyPilot > "Career memory"
    (records per kind, full history, "What changed" timeline).
    Or run:  npm run career:memory  [stats | sync | list <kind> |
             show <id> | timeline]
  * Your real data today: 56 skills, 2 projects, 3 education entries,
    4 experience entries, 4 certifications/achievements, 8 resumes,
    6 applications, 54 screening questions, 32 companies.
  * Included in the privacy export. Deleted only if you tick
    "career memory" in Settings > Privacy > Delete.
  * Tests: 20 new tests (203 of 203 passing at that point).

DONE AND TESTED (1 Oct 2026) - Phase 3: JD Intelligence / JD Decompiler
  * Turns every job description into structured, checkable facts:
      role, company, location, work mode, experience, education,
      seniority, mandatory / preferred / nice-to-have skills,
      responsibilities, certifications, salary,
      and the Indian campus rules: batch, CGPA, percentage, branch,
      bond, notice period. Plus the industry (domain).
  * Every value comes with:
      - the exact words from the JD (or the job listing) and the line
        number they are on,
      - a confidence score (high / medium / low) from a written rule,
      - how it was found.
  * Reports what the JD does NOT say (for example "salary not stated")
    and where the JD contradicts itself (for example "freshers welcome"
    but also "minimum 5 years").
  * Handles messy JDs: HTML pages, one long paragraph, empty or
    placeholder text, very long lines. Nothing crashes.
  * Connected to the pipeline: every time a job is matched, its JD
    intelligence is saved in a new "JD Intelligence" column of the
    Job Details tab. Mandatory skills are always exactly the ones the
    eligibility check uses.
  * Where to see it: ApplyPilot > Jobs > open a job > "JD intelligence".
    Or run:  npm run jd:decompile  (adds it to jobs that don't have it).
  * Your real data: all 125 JDs processed, every quote verified exact,
    average confidence 82%. 3 JDs contradict themselves (Okta,
    Notion, Toast).
  * Fixed along the way: short requirement lines such as "Minimum 5
    years of experience" or "Immediate joiners preferred" were being
    read as section headings, which hid requirements from the matcher.
  * Found, NOT changed: in 15 of the 125 JDs the eligibility check
    cannot read the experience wording (e.g. "IT/software development
    experience"). One of them, MindTickle JOB-000030, asks for 2+ years
    and is marked eligible.
  * Tests: 23 new tests, 225 of 225 passing in total.

DONE AND TESTED (1 Oct 2026) - Phases 4 to 23: the Career Engineering layer
  All new code is in agent/career/ and is shown in ApplyPilot under the
  new "Career" section of the sidebar. Rule everywhere: measure proof,
  never pretend; say KNOWN / INFERRED / LIKELY / UNKNOWN; never claim a
  cause the data can't show.

  Phase 4  Career DNA - an evidence score (0-100) per skill from a fixed
           points table (job 35, internship 30, project 20, training 12,
           certificate 12, course 8, skills list 10, verified evidence 25).
           Each score shows its arithmetic, whether it is verified /
           documented / only claimed, a confidence, and freshness.
           Current DNA vs Target DNA (what your relevant jobs expect),
           plus score history.
  Phase 5  Evidence Graph - You -> Skill -> Evidence (job, internship,
           project, GitHub, deployment, certificate, course, resume).
           Each link has a strength, verification and freshness.
  Phase 6  Readiness - for every job: APPLY NOW / PREPARE FIRST / BUILD
           PROOF FIRST / LEARN FIRST / NETWORK FIRST / WAIT / SKIP /
           NOT ELIGIBLE, with reasons and every component (technical
           match, eligibility, evidence, skill gap, experience gap,
           education incl. batch/CGPA/percentage, location, risk).
           It can be stricter than the eligibility gate, never looser.
           Decision history is kept.
  Phase 7  Career X-Ray - "Analyze my career": readiness, target roles,
           qualified / near-match / stretch jobs, skill, evidence, resume
           and interview gaps, top companies, ranked actions, changes
           since last time. Export as HTML or Markdown.
  Phase 8  Market - demand by skill, role, company, city, experience and
           technology area; daily snapshots and trends; data freshness.
  Phase 9  Learning - ranked skills to learn or prove (with the formula),
           prerequisites, ordered learning path, steps, official
           resources, practice, project, progress.
  Phase 10 Job-to-Project compiler - a full project plan for a gap or a
           job: requirements, architecture, APIs, database, milestones,
           acceptance criteria, tests, deployment, README, evidence
           checklist, interview questions.
  Phase 11 Evidence verification - GitHub ownership, technologies found
           in the code/config, README, tests, live URL + API health +
           hosting platform. Only skills actually found count.
  Phase 12 30/60/90-day mission - phases, weekly goals, today's tasks,
           milestones; ticks itself from real progress; re-plans when
           you fall behind or your top gap changes.
  Phase 13 Resume evolution - every version, its strategy, what changed,
           and what happened (no causal claims).
  Phase 14 Application intelligence - per job: go / caution / stop,
           which resume, which projects to lead with, questions this
           company asked before, your history there, duplicate check.
  Phase 15 Interview prediction - topics labelled LIKELY / POSSIBLE /
           UNKNOWN with the reason; never promised questions.
  Phase 16 Interview memory - record questions, answers, what went
           wrong, feedback, result; question bank, weak areas, company
           topics, predicted-vs-actual accuracy.
  Phase 17 Experiment lab - every application as an experiment, with
           small-sample warnings.
  Phase 18 Rejection forensics - KNOWN facts, LIKELY factors only with
           direct evidence, otherwise UNKNOWN.
  Phase 19 Career analytics - funnel, response/interview/offer rates,
           time to response, by company/role/source, weekly trend.
  Phase 20 Career Twin - one summary of everything (card on Overview).
  Phase 21 Career Command Center - new pages: Career X-Ray, Career
           mission, Career DNA, Learning, Projects, Interview center,
           Companies, Market, Career analytics, Career memory; plus
           "What to do about this job" in every job panel.
  Phase 22 Daily cycle - the daily run now has a "career_intelligence"
           step (after matching, before resumes) and the daily report has
           a "Career intelligence" section; ApplyPilot also re-analyses
           after every job search and apply run.
  Phase 23 Notifications - new qualified job, new skill gap, interview
           coming up, new opportunity at a target company, learning
           milestone, evidence verified, mission behind. Each fires once
           and can be switched off in Settings > Notifications.
           (Email / Telegram / WhatsApp are not built.)

JOBS PAGE UPGRADE (1 Oct 2026)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Jobs are cards (4-6 per row); clicking one opens the job's own page
  (#/jobs/JOB-000123) with Previous / Next through the list you came from.
- "All jobs" is a tick-box filter: New today, Eligible, Ready to apply,
  Needs you, Queued, Applied, Failed, Not eligible, Not matched yet, Saved
  (a job shows if it is in ANY ticked group). Active filters show as chips.
- Your filters, search, sort, page and view are remembered in this browser.
- Views: Cards / By company (one collapsible row per company) / Board.
- "Hide duplicates" (on by default) shows each opening once - same company
  and same role name - with "N postings" on the card.
- Each card shows the readiness decision and up to 3 missing mandatory
  skills; hover for Save / Skip / Open posting.
- Board: Ready to apply -> Needs you -> Applied -> Interview -> Offer ->
  Rejected/closed. Dragging a card to Interview / Offer / Rejected (or the
  "Record outcome" menu) records YOUR outcome in career memory, dated
  today. The agent never moves cards into those columns itself.
- Already-submitted jobs (APPLIED or SUBMISSION_UNVERIFIED) show
  "Already applied" and cannot be applied to again from the UI (the
  runtime also refuses them as DUPLICATE).
- Overview has a "Since yesterday" line; each item opens the matching list.
- API: /api/jobs?groups=a,b&dedupe=1, /api/jobs/summary, /api/jobs/board.
- Tests: 8 new (283 of 283 passing).
